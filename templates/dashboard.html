Data leakage note

G1 and G2 (1st/2nd period grades) are strongly predictive of the final grade G3 by construction. With G1/G2 included, classifier accuracy is {{ (metrics.leakage_analysis.classifier_accuracy_with_G1_G2 * 100) | round(1) }}%; without them it drops to {{ (metrics.leakage_analysis.classifier_accuracy_without_G1_G2 * 100) | round(1) }}%. The deployed model keeps G1/G2 since the product asks for in-progress grades by design — but the gap is reported here for transparency rather than hidden behind one headline number.
